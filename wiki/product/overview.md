# Stagecom Overview

**Status:** current
**Last updated:** 2026-04-21
**Sources:** [[raw/product/PRD.md]], [[raw/design/google-stitch-brief.md]]

Stagecom is a community management platform for improv theaters, comedy theaters, indie venues, and similar community arts spaces. It exists to replace fragmented operations spread across spreadsheets, email, texts, Discord, and social media with one purpose-built system.

## Core Problem

- Producers spend too much time coordinating logistics off-platform.
- Performers lack a clear, privacy-aware source of truth for lineup and scheduling.
- Theater managers and staff need oversight without micromanaging every show.

## Product Shape

Stagecom centralizes:

- theater identity and membership
- theater-owned event creation
- approval and review
- casting and invitations
- schedule and callsheet views
- notifications and operational updates

## Product Character

The sources are consistent that Stagecom should feel:

- community-centered
- operational rather than bureaucratic
- privacy-aware
- theater-specific rather than generic SaaS

The design docs increasingly narrow the target audience toward improv/community spaces rather than traditional institutional theater.

## Current Direction

- Stable product invariants come from the PRD and data model.
- Design authority currently comes from the 2026-04-12 reset/spec set, not from preserving the existing frontend.
- The eventual goal is for Stagecom to become the canonical public event record for a theater even if ticketing remains external.
