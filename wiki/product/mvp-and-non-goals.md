# MVP And Non-Goals

**Status:** current
**Last updated:** 2026-04-21
**Sources:** [[raw/product/PRD.md]], [[raw/design/google-stitch-brief.md]]

## MVP Feature Set

- authentication and profiles
- theater creation and membership
- show and event creation inside theaters
- approval workflow and review queue
- lineup and cast management
- casting modes and invitations
- in-app and email notifications
- program/rundown visibility

## Non-Goals

The locked PRD explicitly excludes:

- internal ticket checkout
- payroll or payouts
- marketing automation
- audition marketplace behavior

Ticketing is expected to remain external in v1, though the product should still become the canonical event record.

## Design Constraint

The design/source bundle repeatedly warns against inventing broader social networking or generic business software areas that are outside the locked MVP.
