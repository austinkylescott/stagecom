# Why Roles Are Contextual, Not Global

**Status:** current
**Last updated:** 2026-04-21
**Sources:** [[raw/product/PRD.md]], [[raw/design/google-stitch-brief.md]], [[raw/specs/2026-03-31/feature-spec-authenticated-work-experience-refinement-v1.md]]

Stagecom models roles contextually because the same person may have different responsibilities across theaters and events.

## Decision

- Theater roles and show roles are separate.
- Product surfaces should increasingly organize around viewer relationship and visibility scope, not a single static persona.

## Reasoning

- A performer may also produce a different event.
- Oversight is theater-scoped, not platform-global.
- Privacy and permission decisions become more coherent when roles stay tied to a theater or show relationship.
